We are writing to let you know about changes to our rates that will take effect on **January 1, 2026**. We wanted to give you plenty of notice so you can plan your 2026 budget.

## What is changing

| | Current (2024-2025) | Starting January 1, 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials (per user per month) | $89 | **$95** | +$6 |
| Professional (per user per month) | $135 | **$145** | +$10 |
| Premier (per user per month) | $185 | **$199** | +$14 |
| Out-of-scope labor, business hours | $150/hour | **$165/hour** | +$15 |
| Out-of-scope labor, after hours, weekends, holidays | $225/hour | **$247.50/hour** | +$22.50 (stays at 1.5x the business-hours rate) |

## What is not changing

- Server management stays at **$175 per server per month**
- Network device management stays at **$25 per device per month**
- The VaultLine backup add-on for Essentials stays at **$6 per user per month**
- Project work stays at **$150 per hour**
- The metro onsite trip charge stays at **$75**
- Billing stays the same: invoices on the 1st of the month, Net 15

## Why rates are changing

This is our first rate change in more than three years. Over the past two years, our costs have gone up in a few areas that directly affect your service:

1. **Security tools.** The endpoint protection, monitoring, and backup products we license on your behalf have all raised their prices, and we have added protections that weren't standard two years ago.
2. **People.** We have added technicians to keep phone answer times short as our client base has grown, and we have invested in training and certifications for our engineers. Keeping experienced, local technicians is the heart of "IT that answers the phone."
3. **Coverage.** We rebuilt our after-hours on-call system this year so that emergency calls go straight to the on-call engineer through a dedicated line, PagerLine, at (913) 555-0142.

## What you are getting in 2026

Along with the new rates, we are clarifying and improving what each tier includes. Our updated 2026 Service Catalog and Service Level Agreement will be sent to you in December. Highlights:

- **Business hours** for our help desk are **7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central Time**, Monday through Friday.
- **Professional** clients receive **4 hours of onsite support per month** in the metro area, included.
- **Professional** backup retention is **30 daily plus 12 monthly** restore points; **Premier** adds **7 yearly** restore points.
- Clearer **SLA credits** for missed P1 response targets.
- To make sure every new employee is ready on day one, we will ask for **5 business days' notice** for new user setups starting in 2026.

## What you need to do

Nothing right now. Your January 1, 2026 invoice will reflect the new rates automatically. Your agreement terms, tier, and services otherwise stay the same.

If you would like to talk through what this means for your budget, or whether a different tier might fit you better, please reach out to your Account Manager. Your vCIO, Jordan Pike, is also happy to review your 2026 plans with you.
