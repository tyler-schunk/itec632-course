## 2. How our tiers work

Every client chooses one of three managed service tiers: **Essentials**, **Professional**, or **Premier**. Tiers are priced **per user, per month**, and every user in your organization is on the same tier. A "user" is a person with a Microsoft 365 account and/or a workstation we manage. Shared mailboxes, conference room accounts, and scanner accounts are not counted as users.

All three tiers include the same core protection: monitoring, patching, endpoint protection, and Microsoft 365 administration. The tiers differ in support hours, backup, onsite visits, after-hours coverage, and how often we meet with you for planning.

## 3. What every tier includes in detail

### 3.1 Remote help desk

Our help desk is staffed by Tier 1 and Tier 2 technicians, backed by Tier 3 systems engineers. You can reach us three ways:

1. **Phone:** (913) 555-0111. Best for anything urgent. **P1 Critical issues must be phoned in.**
2. **Email:** help@larkspurridge.example. Every email creates a ticket automatically.
3. **Client portal:** portal.larkspurridge.example. Submit tickets, add notes, check status, and see your history.

Every request gets a ticket number in the format TCK-123456. Please keep it handy when you call back about the same issue.

### 3.2 Monitoring, patching, and endpoint protection

- The Beacon RMM agent watches disk space, failed services, offline devices, and security alerts around the clock. Alerts create tickets automatically. Alerts that arrive after hours are worked according to your tier's after-hours coverage.
- Windows updates are approved in rings. Most workstations are patched during an overnight maintenance window, and we reboot only when needed. If a user leaves a machine off for weeks, it gets caught up the next time it comes online.
- Endpoint protection is installed and managed by us. Please do not install a second antivirus product; two products fight each other and slow machines down.

### 3.3 Microsoft 365 administration

We manage your Microsoft 365 tenant, including Entra ID, Exchange Online, Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Intune where licensed. This includes:
- Creating and removing users, assigning licenses, and resetting passwords
- MFA enrollment and resets using Microsoft Authenticator with number matching (SMS codes are not accepted for new enrollments)
- Shared mailbox and calendar permissions
- Distribution lists and Teams setup
- Basic mail flow troubleshooting and junk mail tuning

License costs for Microsoft 365 are separate from your Larkspur fees. We can resell licenses or manage licenses you buy directly.

### 3.4 Identity verification

To protect your accounts, we verify identity before resetting passwords or MFA:
- For a password reset, the technician calls the user back at the phone number on file.
- For an MFA reset, the technician calls the user back **and** confirms with your organization's designated authorized contact.
- We never verify by date of birth or Social Security number. Please keep your users' phone numbers and your authorized contact list current. Your Account Manager can update them.

### 3.5 Network device management (add-on)

Firewalls, switches, and wireless access points are managed for $25 per device per month. Managed devices are monitored, backed up (configuration), and kept on current firmware. Most of our clients run Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and Ubiquiti UniFi switches and access points.

### 3.6 Server management (add-on)

Servers (physical or virtual) are managed for $175 per server per month. This includes monitoring, patching, disk space alerts, service health checks, and coordination with VaultLine backup for server workloads.

## 4. Backup (VaultLine)

### 4.1 What is backed up

VaultLine protects:
- Microsoft 365 mailboxes, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams files
- Managed servers (when server management is in place)
- Designated workstations that store data locally (on request; we recommend storing files in OneDrive or SharePoint instead)

### 4.2 Retention by tier

| Tier | Retention |
|---|---|
| Essentials | No backup unless the VaultLine add-on is purchased. With the add-on: **14 daily** restore points. |
| Professional | **30 daily + 12 monthly** restore points |
| Premier | **30 daily + 12 monthly + 7 yearly** restore points |

"30 daily" means we can restore a file as it existed on any of the last 30 days. "12 monthly" means we also keep one restore point per month for the last 12 months. "7 yearly" means one restore point per year for 7 years.

### 4.3 Restore requests

Submit a ticket with the file or folder name, its location, and roughly when it was last correct. Restores are usually P3 (single user) or P2 (multiple users or a key shared folder). If you need a whole mailbox or a whole server restored, call us so we can talk through the plan.

### 4.4 Backup monitoring

We check backup job results every business day and work failures as tickets. Your Account Manager can provide a backup status summary on request.

## 5. Onsite support

### 5.1 Metro area

Our metro area is the Kansas City metro, roughly within 40 miles of our Lenexa office. This covers our clients in Johnson, Wyandotte, Jackson, Clay, and Douglas counties. Visits outside the metro are quoted ahead of time.

### 5.2 Onsite by tier

- **Essentials:** onsite visits are billable at the out-of-scope labor rate ($165/hour business hours, 15-minute increments) plus a $75 trip charge within the metro.
- **Professional:** **4 hours of onsite time per month are included** in the metro. Time beyond 4 hours in a month is billable at the out-of-scope rate, plus a $75 trip charge for each additional billable visit. Unused hours do not roll over to the next month. Your Account Manager can provide your monthly onsite-hours usage on request.
- **Premier:** unlimited onsite support in the metro. No trip charges in the metro.

### 5.3 How onsite visits are scheduled

Most issues are fixed remotely, and we try remote first because it is faster for you. When a technician decides a visit is needed (or you request one), our dispatcher, Isaac Brennan, schedules it with your office contact. Our field team is led by Brian Novak, with Olivia Grant and Derek Simmons handling most visits.

Onsite time is counted from arrival to departure. Travel time inside the metro is not counted against your included hours or billed.

### 5.4 What onsite does not cover

Onsite support is for IT issues on equipment we manage. It does not include moving furniture, pulling new network cable through walls, or installing hardware we did not supply. We are happy to quote those as projects or refer you to a trusted vendor.

## 6. New users, changes, and offboarding

### 6.1 New user setup (onboarding)

Please submit new user requests **at least 5 business days before the person's start date**. Use the "New User" form in the portal or email help@larkspurridge.example with:

- Full name and preferred display name
- Job title and department
- Start date
- Manager
- Which shared mailboxes, Teams, and shared folders they need
- Whether they need a new computer, phone extension, or printer access
- Mobile phone number for MFA enrollment and identity callbacks

Five business days gives us time to create the account, assign licenses, prepare or order a computer, and test everything before day one. If we get a request with less notice, we will do our best, but we cannot guarantee the person will be ready on their first morning. If new hardware needs to be ordered, allow more time; shipping is outside our control.

New user setup is a P4 request and is included in all tiers (remote work). If a computer needs to be set up onsite, that time follows your tier's onsite rules.

### 6.2 Changes

Permission changes, adding someone to a shared mailbox, installing approved software, and similar small changes are included in all tiers. Software must be licensed by you; we will tell you if something needs a license.

### 6.3 Offboarding

When an employee leaves, we disable their account, revoke sessions, remove MFA methods, forward or convert their mailbox as you direct, and collect a list of devices to recover.

- Requests received **before 3:00 PM CT on a business day are completed the same business day**.
- Requests received after 3:00 PM CT are completed the next business day, unless you need it sooner. For a sensitive departure (for example, a termination happening right now), **call us** and we will treat it as urgent.
- Please tell us what should happen to the person's email and files (forward to manager, convert to shared mailbox, keep for a set period, etc.).

## 10. What is out of scope

Some work is not covered by any tier's monthly fee. Out-of-scope work is billed at **$165/hour** during business hours or **$247.50/hour** after hours, on weekends, and on holidays, or quoted as a project at **$150/hour**. We will always tell you before starting work that will be billed.

Examples of out-of-scope or project work:
- Office moves, new office buildouts, and network cabling
- Migrations (for example, moving from an old file server to SharePoint, or switching email providers)
- Deploying more than a handful of new computers at once (a hardware refresh project)
- Training sessions for groups of staff
- Support for personal devices, home networks, or family members' computers
- Line-of-business software that the software vendor is responsible for (we will happily open the vendor ticket and sit in on the call, but fixing their product is their job)
- Recovering data from devices that were not covered by VaultLine backup
- Cleanup after a user installs unapproved software against our advice, when the cleanup takes more than an hour
- Website hosting, website design, and social media accounts
- Phone system configuration for systems we do not manage
- Printer hardware repair (we troubleshoot and coordinate with your printer vendor or leasing company)
- Work requested at a specific after-hours time by Essentials or Professional clients for non-P1 issues (for example, "please update the server Saturday night")

Projects are quoted in writing before work begins. Our projects engineer, Rachel Kim, handles most migrations and deployments.
