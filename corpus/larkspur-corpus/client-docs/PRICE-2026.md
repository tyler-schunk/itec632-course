# Larkspur Ridge IT - 2026 Price Sheet

**Effective January 1, 2026.** These prices apply to all invoices dated on or after January 1, 2026.

Questions about pricing: your Account Manager (Alicia Brandt or Kevin Duong) or Samuel Ortiz, Controller, at (913) 555-0100 or billing@larkspurridge.example.

---

## 1. Managed service tiers

Billed per user, per month. All users in an organization are on the same tier.

| Tier | Price per user per month | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | **$95.00** | Business-hours help desk; monitoring, patching, endpoint protection; Microsoft 365 administration; annual business review |
| **Professional** | **$145.00** | Everything in Essentials, plus VaultLine backup (30 daily + 12 monthly), 24x7 for P1, 4 onsite hours/month included, quarterly business review |
| **Premier** | **$199.00** | Everything in Professional, plus 24x7 for all priorities, unlimited metro onsite, after-hours work included, 7 yearly backups, monthly business review |

See the Service Catalog (SVC-CAT-2026) for full details of what each tier includes.

## 3. Labor rates

| Type | Rate | Billing increment |
|---|---|---|
| Out-of-scope labor, business hours (Mon-Fri 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM CT) | **$165.00/hour** | 15-minute increments |
| Out-of-scope labor, after hours, weekends, and holidays | **$247.50/hour** (1.5x) | 1-hour minimum, then 15-minute increments |

## 5. Hardware and licensing

Hardware (computers, firewalls, access points, phones) and software licenses (including Microsoft 365) are quoted separately at the time of purchase. Our 2026 standard laptop is the Dell Latitude 5550 (Intel Core Ultra 5, 32 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, 3-year ProSupport warranty), and our standard desktop is the Dell OptiPlex Micro 7020 with 16 GB RAM. Your Account Manager will provide current quotes, because hardware prices change often.

---

## 6. Billing terms

- Managed-services fees and add-ons are billed monthly, in advance, on the **1st of each month**.
- Payment terms: **Net 15**.
- Out-of-scope labor, after-hours labor, and trip charges are billed in arrears on the next monthly invoice.
- Projects are billed according to the project quote.
- User counts are updated monthly based on active users on the last business day of the prior month.
