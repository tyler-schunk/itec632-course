# Master Services Agreement: Plain-Language Summary

**Effective January 1, 2026**

This one-page summary explains the key terms of the Larkspur Ridge IT Master Services Agreement (MSA) in everyday language. It is here to help, not to replace the agreement. If this summary and your signed MSA ever disagree, **your signed MSA controls**.

---

### What the agreement covers

The MSA is the main contract between your organization and Larkspur Ridge IT, LLC. It has three exhibits:

- **Exhibit A - Service Catalog** (SVC-CAT-2026): what your tier includes and what is out of scope
- **Exhibit B - Service Level Agreement** (SLA-EXH-2026): priorities, response and resolution targets, and SLA credits
- **Exhibit C - Pricing** (PRICE-2026): your tier price, add-ons, and labor rates

### Term and renewal

- Your **initial term** is the length shown on your signed order form (most clients choose 12 months).
- After that, the agreement **renews automatically for 12-month terms** unless either side gives notice.

### Ending the agreement

- Either party may end the agreement, or choose not to renew, with **60 days' written notice**.
- Email or a letter to your Account Manager counts as written notice.
- If you leave, we help with an orderly handoff: we provide your documentation, admin credentials, and assistance transitioning to your new provider. Offboarding help beyond a normal handoff is billed at our standard rates.
- Fees for the 60-day notice period still apply.

### Billing and payment

- Monthly managed-services fees and add-ons are **billed in advance on the 1st of each month**.
- Payment is due **Net 15** (within 15 days of the invoice date).
- Out-of-scope labor, after-hours labor (when not included in your tier), and trip charges are billed on the next monthly invoice.
- Your user count is updated each month. Adding users raises your next invoice; removing users lowers it.
- Accounts more than 30 days past due may have non-emergency work paused until the balance is current, and SLA targets do not apply during that time.

### Price changes

- We may change prices with written notice before the change takes effect. For example, 2026 rates were announced on November 3, 2025, for January 1, 2026.

### Changing tiers

- You can move to a different tier at any time. Changes take effect on the 1st of the next billing month.

### Service levels and credits

- Response and resolution targets are in Exhibit B.
- If we miss a **P1 response target**, you receive a credit of **5% of that month's managed-services fee per miss, up to 20%** per month. Resolution targets are goals and are not credited.
- **P1 Critical issues must be phoned in** for SLA targets to apply.

### Your responsibilities

- Name at least one authorized contact who can approve MFA resets, purchases, and access changes
- Keep user contact information current
- Give at least **5 business days' notice** for new users
- Let us install and run our monitoring agent on company computers
- Pay invoices on time

### Confidentiality and data

- We keep your information confidential and only access your systems to provide the services.
- Your data belongs to you. When the agreement ends, we return or help transfer it as you direct.
