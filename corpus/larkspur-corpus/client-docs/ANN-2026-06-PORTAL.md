# Introducing the New Larkspur Client Portal

**June 15, 2026**

Hello from Larkspur Ridge IT,

We're excited to share that our client portal at **portal.larkspurridge.example** has a fresh look and several new features, starting today. Many of these came straight from your suggestions at business reviews, so thank you for telling us what would make your life easier.

Your login stays the same. Just visit the portal and sign in with your work Microsoft 365 account like before.

## What's new

### 1. Live ticket status with the "Where's my ticket?" timeline

Every ticket now shows a simple timeline: **Received, Assigned, In Progress, Waiting on You, Resolved**. You can see which technician has your ticket and when they last updated it.

If a ticket says **Waiting on You**, it means we need something from you (an answer, an approval, or a time to connect). The SLA clock pauses while a ticket is waiting on you, so replying quickly gets things moving again. You can reply right from the ticket page.

### 2. Smarter "New User" form

The New User form now walks you through everything we need in one go: name, title, start date, manager, mailboxes, Teams, shared folders, hardware, and a mobile number for MFA.

The form also shows the **earliest start date we can guarantee**, which is **5 business days** from today (not counting weekends and holidays). You can still pick a sooner date, and we'll do our best, but the form will let you know we can't guarantee everything will be ready on day one.

### 3. "Someone is leaving" form

A new offboarding form lets you tell us exactly what to do with a departing employee's account, email, files, and devices. Requests submitted **before 3:00 PM CT on a business day are completed the same business day**. For urgent or sensitive departures, the form reminds you to call us at (913) 555-0111 as well.

### 4. Onsite hours tracker (Professional clients)

Professional clients include **4 hours of onsite support per month**. The new **Onsite** page shows how many of this month's included hours you've used, with a list of each visit, the technician, and arrival and departure times. Hours reset on the 1st of each month and do not roll over.

Premier clients will see their visit history too (Premier onsite in the metro is unlimited). Essentials clients will see billable visits and their charges.

### 5. Backup status at a glance

Professional and Premier clients, and Essentials clients with the VaultLine add-on, can now see a simple **Backups** page showing whether last night's backups succeeded, plus a reminder of your retention:

- Essentials (with add-on): 14 daily restore points
- Professional: 30 daily + 12 monthly
- Premier: 30 daily + 12 monthly + 7 yearly

There's also a new **Restore Request** button that asks for the file name, location, and when it was last correct, so we can get to work right away.

### 6. Invoices and user counts

Authorized contacts can now view and download invoices, see the user count we billed for each month, and compare it to the list of active users. If something doesn't match, there's a "Question this invoice" button that goes straight to our billing team.

### 7. Asset list

See every computer, firewall, switch, access point, and printer we manage for you, with its asset tag (like LR-ABC-0123), model, assigned user, and warranty end date. Computers coming up on warranty end are highlighted so you can budget ahead. This is the same list Jordan Pike brings to your business reviews.

### 8. Knowledge articles for everyday questions

We've added short how-to articles written for your staff, not for technicians: connecting to guest Wi-Fi, setting up Authenticator on a new phone, fixing Teams audio, sharing a OneDrive file, and more.

## What hasn't changed

- **P1 Critical issues must still be phoned in.** Please don't use the portal for business-down emergencies. Call **(913) 555-0111** during business hours or **PagerLine at (913) 555-0142** after hours. The portal isn't watched in real time after hours.
- You can still email **help@larkspurridge.example** anytime. Emails create tickets just like before and show up in the portal.
- Our business hours are still **Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM CT**.

## Who can see what?

- **All users** can submit tickets and see their own tickets.
- **Authorized contacts** can see all of their organization's tickets, invoices, the asset list, and the onsite and backup pages, and they can submit New User and offboarding forms.

If you need to change who your authorized contacts are, ask your Account Manager.
