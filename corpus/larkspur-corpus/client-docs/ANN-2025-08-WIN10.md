# Windows 10 End of Support: What It Means for Your Office

**August 12, 2025**

Hello from Larkspur Ridge IT,

You may have heard that Microsoft is ending support for Windows 10. We want to explain what that means in plain language, where your office stands, and what we're doing to help you get ready.

## The short version

- **Microsoft will stop supporting Windows 10 on October 14, 2025.**
- After that date, Windows 10 computers will stop receiving security updates. They will still turn on and work, but every month they will become a little less safe.
- Our goal is to have **every managed computer on Windows 11** before October 14, or to have a clear plan for the few that can't make it in time.
- Many of you have already upgraded or replaced most of your computers. Thank you! If your office still has Windows 10 machines, we will be in touch (or already have been) with a specific plan.

## Why this matters

Security updates are how Microsoft fixes holes that attackers find in Windows. Once updates stop, any new hole found in Windows 10 stays open forever. Attackers know this, and older systems become easy targets.

There are business reasons too:
- Many software vendors (accounting, practice management, design tools) stop supporting their products on Windows 10 soon after Microsoft does.
- Cyber insurance applications increasingly ask whether you run unsupported operating systems.
- Some industry regulations expect systems to be kept up to date.

## What we're doing

### 1. Inventory (done)

Using our Beacon RMM monitoring, we have a list of every managed computer at your office, its age, its Windows version, and whether it can run Windows 11. Your vCIO, Jordan Pike, or your Account Manager has this list and can send it to you anytime.

### 2. Free upgrades for computers that qualify

If a computer meets Microsoft's Windows 11 requirements (generally, computers bought in roughly 2019 or later), we can upgrade it to Windows 11 in place. Files and programs stay where they are.

**For managed computers, the Windows 11 upgrade is included in your monthly service.** We schedule upgrades remotely, usually overnight or at a time you choose, one group at a time so your office is never all down at once. The upgrade takes about 1 to 2 hours per computer.

### 3. Replacement for computers that don't qualify

Computers that can't run Windows 11 need to be replaced. We'll send you a quote for each office with replacement machines. Our current standard laptop is:

**Dell Latitude 5440**
- Intel Core i5 processor
- **16 GB RAM**
- 512 GB SSD
- 3-year Dell ProSupport warranty
- Windows 11 Pro

We also offer a standard Dell OptiPlex Micro desktop for front desk and shop floor stations. If someone needs more power (for example, CAD, video editing, or large spreadsheets), we'll recommend an upgraded model.

Setting up a replacement computer for an individual user is part of your normal service. If your office is replacing many computers at once, we'll quote it as a small refresh project so we can schedule enough technician time to do it right.

### 4. Business software check

Before upgrading, we check that your line-of-business software supports Windows 11. In a few cases, the software vendor needs to provide an update first. We'll coordinate with them.

## What if we can't replace everything in time?

We understand budgets are real. If a few computers can't be replaced by October 14:

- Microsoft offers paid **Extended Security Updates (ESU)** for Windows 10 for a limited time. This buys time, but it costs money per computer and doesn't last forever. We can help you enroll if it makes sense.
- We will make sure those computers keep their endpoint protection and are watched closely.
- We'll recommend limiting what they're used for (for example, not using them for email or banking).

We don't recommend running Windows 10 long term, and we'll keep bringing it up at your business reviews until it's resolved.
