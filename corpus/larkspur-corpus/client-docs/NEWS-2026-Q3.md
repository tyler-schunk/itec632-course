## 2. Ticket-writing refresher: the "what changed?" question

*By Priya Raman, Service Desk Manager*

Last quarter we shared the five things every great ticket has. This time, I want to zoom in on the one that people skip most often: **what changed?**

When something that worked yesterday doesn't work today, something changed. Often it's something small that you might not think is related:

- "I moved to a different desk this morning." (Different network jack, different dock.)
- "The power flickered last night." (Printer or switch may have come back with a new address.)
- "I changed my password yesterday." (Phones, tablets, and scanners may still have the old one saved.)
- "I installed a new app my client sent me." (Could conflict with something else.)
- "I got a new phone." (Authenticator needs to move over.)

Telling us what changed can turn an hour of troubleshooting into five minutes. If nothing changed that you know of, say that too. It's useful information!

### A real-world example (shared with permission)

> "Scanning to email from the upstairs copier stopped working this morning. Downstairs copier is fine. I changed the scanner account password Friday because it expired."

Our technician knew exactly where to look. The copier still had the old password saved. Fixed in ten minutes.

## 4. Summer travel tips for laptops

Taking a work laptop on vacation (or to a conference)? A few tips:

- **Don't leave it in a hot car.** Heat is hard on batteries and screens. A Kansas summer car can top 130 degrees inside.
- **Use your phone's hotspot instead of hotel or airport Wi-Fi** when you can. If you must use public Wi-Fi, connect to your company VPN if you have one.
- **Lost or stolen? Call us right away** at (913) 555-0111 (or PagerLine after hours if you're on Premier). We can lock the device and sign it out of Microsoft 365.
- **Bring your charger** and a backup if you can. A surprising number of after-hours calls are really "I left my charger at the office."
- **Restart before you leave.** Let any pending updates finish at the office, not at the gate.

## 5. Guest Wi-Fi: keep visitors off your business network

Most of our clients have a separate **guest Wi-Fi network**. It gives visitors, patients, and customers internet access without letting them see your computers, printers, or server.

Good habits:
- Give visitors the **guest** network, never your main business network.
- Change the guest password every so often (we can do this for you; just submit a ticket).
- Don't connect company laptops to the guest network. It won't reach your printers or shared files.

Don't have a guest network yet? If you have UniFi Wi-Fi managed by us, it's usually a quick change. Submit a ticket and we'll take care of it.
