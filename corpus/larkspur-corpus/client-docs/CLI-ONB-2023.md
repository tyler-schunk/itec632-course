# Welcome to Larkspur Ridge IT!
## Your First 90 Days: A Client Onboarding Guide

Thank you for choosing Larkspur Ridge IT. We know switching IT providers can feel like a big step, and we want it to be smooth, predictable, and as low-stress as possible for you and your team.

This guide explains what happens during your first 90 days with us, what we will need from you, and what you can expect along the way. Keep it handy, and share it with whoever at your office will be our main point of contact.

If you have questions at any point, call us at **(913) 555-0100** or email **help@larkspurridge.example**.

## 4. Kickoff (Week 0)

Your kickoff meeting is usually 60 to 90 minutes, at your office or on Teams. Jordan Pike runs it, with Rachel Kim and your Account Manager.

We will cover:
- Your business: how you work, your busiest times, the software you can't live without
- Who your **authorized contacts** are (people who can approve purchases, new accounts, and access changes)
- Any known problems or upcoming deadlines we should know about
- The transition plan from your previous IT provider
- How your staff will contact us

### What we need from you at kickoff

Please gather as much of this as you can. Don't worry if you don't have it all; finding the missing pieces is part of our job.

- [ ] A list of your employees, with email addresses, phone numbers, and job titles
- [ ] Your authorized contacts (at least one, ideally two)
- [ ] Admin access to Microsoft 365 (or your previous provider's contact so we can request it)
- [ ] Your domain registrar login (where your website address is registered)
- [ ] Internet provider account info and account number
- [ ] Login info or contacts for your firewall, Wi-Fi, and any servers
- [ ] A list of business software and the vendor support contacts for each
- [ ] Printer or copier leasing company contact info
- [ ] Any warranty or purchase records for computers

### Working with your previous provider

We handle the conversation with your previous IT provider for you. Most providers are professional about handoffs. We will ask for passwords, documentation, and any licenses they hold on your behalf. If your previous provider is slow to respond, we may ask you to contact them as the account owner, since they work for you.

## 5. Discovery (Weeks 1-3)

During discovery, we learn everything about your technology so we can support it properly.

### 5.1 Agent installation

We install the **Beacon RMM** agent on every company computer and server. This lightweight program lets us monitor health, install updates, and connect remotely when you ask for help. We also install our managed endpoint protection and remove your old antivirus if needed.

Most agent installs happen remotely and in the background. Users may see a brief pop-up or be asked to restart once. For computers that are hard to reach remotely, Brian Novak or one of our field technicians will visit.

### 5.2 Site walkthrough

A field technician will visit your office to look at your network closet, firewall, switches, Wi-Fi, printers, and phones. We'll take photos and label equipment with Larkspur asset tags (they look like LR-ABC-0001). This visit usually takes 1 to 3 hours.

### 5.3 Documentation

Everything we learn goes into our internal documentation system, so any technician who helps you will know how your office is set up. This includes your network layout, important passwords (stored securely), vendor contacts, and any special instructions ("the front desk computer must never restart during business hours," for example).

### 5.4 Microsoft 365 review

Renee Castillo reviews your Microsoft 365 setup, including licenses, admin accounts, security settings, shared mailboxes, and MFA. We often find unused licenses we can remove, which saves you money.

## 6. Stabilize (Weeks 3-6)

Once we understand your environment, we fix the most important things first.

Common stabilization work includes:
- Turning on **MFA** for all users (we'll help each person enroll; it takes about 5 minutes per person)
- Setting up **VaultLine backup** for Microsoft 365 and servers (Professional and Premier, or Essentials with the backup add-on)
- Catching up on missing Windows updates
- Updating firewall firmware and reviewing firewall rules
- Removing old user accounts from people who no longer work for you
- Replacing any unsupported equipment that is a real risk (we'll discuss this with you first; we never buy anything without your approval)

Some of this work is covered by your onboarding project quote. If we find something big that wasn't expected, such as a failing server, we'll talk with you and agree on a plan before doing anything.

Go-live is the day your staff officially start calling Larkspur for help. We'll pick the date together at kickoff.

### Before go-live, we will send you:
- A one-page "How to Get Help" sheet to print or share with staff
- A short welcome email you can forward to your team
- Client portal logins for your authorized contacts

### How your staff get help

- **Phone:** (913) 555-0111 (best for anything urgent)
- **Email:** help@larkspurridge.example
- **Portal:** portal.larkspurridge.example

Every request gets a ticket number. For true emergencies, where the business is down, **always call**.

### The first few weeks of support

Expect a busier-than-normal ticket volume right after go-live. That's healthy. Staff often save up small annoyances ("my second monitor flickers," "I never got access to the shared calendar") and now have someone to tell. We'd much rather hear about these early.

## 8. Adding and removing users

### New employees

Please give us at least **3 business days' notice** before a new employee's start date. Use the "New User" form in the portal or email help@larkspurridge.example. We'll need:

- Full name and job title
- Start date and manager
- Mailboxes, shared folders, and Teams they need access to
- Whether they need a computer, phone, or printer setup
- A mobile phone number

With 3 business days, we can create the account, assign a license, prepare a computer, and make sure everything works on day one. If you need a new computer ordered, tell us as early as possible so shipping doesn't hold things up.

### Departing employees

Tell us as soon as you know. We disable the account, secure the mailbox, and collect a list of their equipment. Requests received before 3:00 PM on a business day are completed the same day. For sensitive departures, call us so we can act immediately.

## 9. Optimize (Weeks 6-12)

With the urgent work done and support running, we turn to longer-term improvements:

- Reviewing computer ages and warranties, and building a replacement schedule
- Cleaning up file shares and moving files into OneDrive or SharePoint where it makes sense
- Fine-tuning Wi-Fi and printers
- Reviewing user feedback and ticket trends from the first weeks
- Wrapping up the onboarding checklist and confirming everything is documented

## 10. Your first business review (around Day 90)

Jordan Pike will meet with you to review how onboarding went and where to go next. We'll bring:

- A summary of tickets so far: how many, what types, and how quickly we responded
- Your hardware inventory with ages and warranty status
- Security status: MFA, backups, patching, endpoint protection
- A recommended roadmap and budget for the next 12 months

After that, reviews happen on your plan's schedule: annually for Essentials, quarterly for Professional, and monthly for Premier.

## 11. Tips for a smooth start

1. **Name a point person.** One person at your office who knows what's going on makes everything faster.
2. **Tell your staff we're coming.** A quick note from leadership helps people trust the new help desk number.
3. **Report everything.** Small problems are still problems. Submit a ticket even for "minor" annoyances.
4. **Call for emergencies.** Email isn't watched every minute.
5. **Plan ahead for new hires.** Three business days' notice makes day one great.
6. **Ask questions.** If something we do doesn't make sense, ask. We'd rather explain than leave you guessing.
