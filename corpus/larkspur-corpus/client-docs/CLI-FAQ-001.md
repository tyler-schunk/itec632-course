# Larkspur Ridge IT - Client FAQ

*Last updated June 1, 2024*

Welcome! This FAQ answers the questions we hear most often from the people we support. If you don't see your question here, just call us at **(913) 555-0111** or email **help@larkspurridge.example**. We're happy to help, and no question is too small.

---

## Getting help

### 1. How do I get help from Larkspur?

You have three options:

- **Call** the support line at **(913) 555-0111**. A real person answers during business hours.
- **Email** help@larkspurridge.example. Every email automatically creates a ticket.
- **Use the client portal** at portal.larkspurridge.example to submit and track tickets.

For anything urgent, please call. Phone calls always get the fastest attention.

### 2. What are your business hours?

Our help desk is open **Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time**, except on company holidays. Outside those hours, emergency support is available depending on your service plan (see "After hours and holidays" below).

### 3. What is a ticket number and why do I need it?

Every request gets a ticket number that looks like **TCK-102345**. You'll see it in the confirmation email we send. If you call or write back about the same issue, give us the ticket number so we can pick up right where we left off instead of starting over.

### 4. I sent an email but haven't heard back. Did you get it?

You should receive an automatic confirmation email with a ticket number within a few minutes. If you didn't, check your junk folder first, then give us a call. The confirmation is automatic; a technician will follow up personally based on the priority of your request.

### 5. How do I write a good ticket?

The more we know up front, the faster we can fix it. A great ticket includes:

- **Who** is affected (just you, your team, the whole office?)
- **What** is happening, in your own words, and what you were trying to do
- **When** it started, and whether anything changed recently (new software, moved desks, power outage)
- **Error messages** - a photo from your phone is perfect
- **How to reach you** - the best phone number for a callback

"Outlook says 'disconnected' since this morning, just me, I can still use webmail" is much more helpful than "email broken."

### 6. Can I call a technician directly?

Please call the main support line instead of a technician's direct number. Our technicians are often on other calls or onsite, and the support line makes sure someone available picks up your request right away.

## Priorities and response times

### 7. What counts as urgent?

We sort every request into one of four priorities:

- **P1 Critical** - the business is down, or most people can't work. Examples: the internet is out at the office, the server is down, or nobody can get email.
- **P2 High** - several people are affected, or something important isn't working right. Also, if a key executive is completely unable to work.
- **P3 Normal** - one person has a problem, and there may be a workaround.
- **P4 Low** - requests and questions, like setting up a new user, adding someone to a shared mailbox, or "how do I..." questions.

### 8. How fast will you respond?

Response times depend on the priority and your service plan. For example, on the **Essentials** plan we respond to **P1 issues within 1 hour** and **P2 issues within 4 business hours**. Professional and Premier plans have faster response times. Your exact targets are in your Service Level Agreement, and your Account Manager can walk you through them anytime.

"Response" means a technician has personally contacted you and started working on the issue, not just the automatic email.

### 9. Should I email or call for an emergency?

**Call.** P1 issues must be phoned in. Email is not watched every minute, especially outside business hours, and if your internet is down your email may not reach us anyway. Call from a cell phone if you need to.

### 10. I think my ticket has the wrong priority. What should I do?

Call the support line and let us know. Things change - maybe more people are now affected, or a deadline is coming up. You can also ask for Greg Halvorsen, our Service Desk Lead, or Priya Raman, our Service Desk Manager.

## Passwords, accounts, and sign-in

### 11. I forgot my password. What do I do?

Call the support line. For your security, a technician will verify your identity before resetting it, and then walk you through setting a new password. Please don't email your password to anyone, including us. We will never ask you for your current password.

### 12. My account is locked. Is that the same as a forgotten password?

Close. Accounts usually lock after several wrong password attempts. Call us and we can unlock it, usually in just a few minutes. If you didn't make those attempts yourself, tell the technician; we'll take a closer look.

### 13. What is MFA and why do I have to use it?

MFA (multi-factor authentication) means signing in takes two things: your password plus a second check, usually a prompt on your phone. Even if someone steals your password, they can't get in without your phone. We require MFA on all Microsoft 365 accounts. We recommend the free Microsoft Authenticator app.

### 14. I got a new phone. How do I move my MFA?

Before you trade in your old phone, call us if you can. If the old phone is already gone, call us anyway and we'll reset your MFA after verifying who you are, then help you set it up on the new phone.

## New hires and departures

### 15. How much notice do you need for a new employee?

Please give us at least **3 business days' notice** before the new person's start date. That gives us time to create their account, assign licenses, get their computer ready, and test everything so they can start working on day one. You can use the "New User" form in the portal or email us.

If new hardware has to be ordered, please let us know even earlier, since shipping times vary.

### 16. What information do you need for a new user?

Full name, job title, start date, manager, what shared mailboxes or folders they need, whether they need a computer or phone, and a mobile number. The portal form asks for all of it.

### 17. Someone is leaving. What should we do?

Let us know as soon as you can. Requests received **before 3:00 PM on a business day are completed the same day**. Tell us what to do with their email and files (forward to their manager, keep as a shared mailbox, and so on). If the departure is sensitive or happening right now, call us instead of emailing.

## Onsite visits

### 18. Will someone come to our office?

Most issues can be fixed remotely, and remote is usually faster for you. When an issue needs hands-on help, our dispatcher will schedule a technician to come out. Premier clients have onsite visits in the metro included. For other plans, onsite time is handled according to your agreement, and we will always tell you ahead of time if a visit will be billed.

### 19. How do I schedule an onsite visit?

Submit a ticket or call the support line and mention that you'd like someone onsite. Our dispatcher will contact you with available times.

## Backups and files

### 20. Are my files backed up?

If your plan includes VaultLine backup, we back up your Microsoft 365 email, OneDrive, SharePoint, and any servers we manage. On the **Professional** plan, we keep **14 days** of daily backups. Premier clients have longer retention. Essentials clients can add backup for a small per-user fee.

Files saved only on your computer's desktop are usually not backed up. Save your work in OneDrive or SharePoint.

### 21. I deleted a file by accident. Can you get it back?

Often, yes! First check your Recycle Bin and the OneDrive or SharePoint recycle bin (both keep deleted files for a while). If it's not there, submit a ticket with the file name, where it was saved, and about when it was last correct. We'll restore it from backup if it's within your retention period.

### 22. How long does a restore take?

Most single-file restores are done the same day. Larger restores (a whole folder, a mailbox) can take longer depending on size. We'll give you an estimate when we start.

## After hours and holidays

### 23. What if something breaks at night or on the weekend?

Call the support line at (913) 555-0111 and follow the prompts for an after-hours emergency. That pages our on-call technician. Depending on your plan:

- **Premier** - 24x7 support is included.
- **Professional** - P1 critical issues are covered 24x7.
- **Essentials** - after-hours help is available but billable.

### 24. How much does after-hours work cost?

When after-hours work isn't included in your plan, it's billed at **$225 per hour**, with a 1-hour minimum. That includes evenings, weekends, and holidays. We'll always confirm with you before starting billable after-hours work.

### 25. What holidays are you closed?

New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, Christmas Eve (we close at noon), and Christmas Day. We send a reminder before each holiday.

## Billing

### 26. When will I get my invoice?

Invoices go out on the **1st of each month** for that month's services. Payment is due **Net 15**. Out-of-scope or after-hours work from the previous month appears on the same invoice.

### 27. Why is there an extra charge on my invoice?

Usually this is out-of-scope work (like a project or after-hours work that isn't included in your plan) or a change in user count. Business-hours out-of-scope work is billed at $150 per hour in 15-minute increments. If something looks wrong, email billing questions to your Account Manager or call the main line at (913) 555-0100 and ask for billing. We're glad to explain any line item.

### 28. How do you count users?

A user is a person with a Microsoft 365 account or a computer we manage. Shared mailboxes and conference room accounts don't count. We update your user count each month.

## Microsoft 365, printers, Wi-Fi, and everyday stuff

### 29. Outlook is slow or says my mailbox is full. What now?

Call or submit a ticket. We can set up an online archive so older mail moves out of your main mailbox automatically. In the meantime, emptying your Deleted Items folder can free up some space.

### 30. Teams audio isn't working in my meetings.

Check that the right headset or speaker is selected in Teams under Settings > Devices, and that your headset is plugged in before you join. If it still doesn't work, submit a ticket and we'll connect remotely.

### 31. My printer says it's offline.

Try turning the printer off and back on, and make sure it's plugged in and connected to the network (check the display for an IP address or Wi-Fi symbol). If it still shows offline on your computer, submit a ticket. Please don't remove and re-add printers yourself; that can make things harder to fix.

### 32. Scanning to email stopped working.

This often happens after a password change or a Microsoft security update. Submit a ticket and tell us which copier or printer you're using. We'll check the settings and get it going again.

### 33. Can visitors use our Wi-Fi?

Yes - most of our clients have a separate **guest Wi-Fi network** that keeps visitors off your business network. Ask your office manager for the guest password, or submit a ticket if you'd like it changed. If you don't have a guest network, we can set one up.

### 34. Can I install software on my computer?

Submit a ticket with the name of the software and why you need it. We'll check that it's safe and properly licensed and install it for you, usually remotely. Please don't install software from random websites.

### 35. I got a suspicious email. What should I do?

Don't click any links or open attachments. Use the "Report" button in Outlook if you have it, or forward the email to help@larkspurridge.example as an attachment. If you already clicked something, call us right away. No one will be in trouble - the sooner we know, the easier it is to fix.

### 36. I lost my laptop or phone.

Call us right away. We can lock or wipe a company device and sign it out of Microsoft 365 to protect your data.
