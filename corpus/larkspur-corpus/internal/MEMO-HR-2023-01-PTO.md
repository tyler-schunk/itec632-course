Happy New Year, everyone. As part of the 2023 handbook update, we have finalized the paid time off (PTO) accrual schedule for this year. This memo explains the schedule and a few related reminders. Please read it and keep it with your copy of the handbook.

## 1. Accrual Schedule

Effective with the first pay period of 2023, full-time employees accrue PTO according to the following schedule:

| Length of service | Annual PTO | Accrual per biweekly pay period (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| First 2 years | **10 days** (80 hours) | 3.08 hours |
| 3 - 5 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 4.62 hours |
| 6 or more years | 20 days (160 hours) | 6.15 hours |

In short: **new staff accrue 10 days of PTO per year during their first two years of employment.** The higher rate begins with the pay period that includes your service anniversary.

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis.

## 2. Using PTO

- PTO may be used after your first 30 days of employment.
- Submit requests at least two weeks in advance for planned time off.
- Technicians on the on-call schedule must arrange a swap before PTO for that week can be approved.
- If you are out sick, notify your manager and the service desk Teams channel before your shift starts.

## 3. Carryover

You may carry over up to five (5) days of unused PTO into the following year. Any balance above five days on December 31 is forfeited unless state law requires otherwise.

## 4. Checking Your Balance

Your current PTO balance appears on each pay stub in the payroll portal. If you believe your balance is incorrect, contact Samuel Ortiz or me.

## 5. Questions

Please contact me with any questions about this memo or your PTO balance.
