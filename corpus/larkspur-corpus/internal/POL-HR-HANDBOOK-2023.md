# Larkspur Ridge IT, LLC - Employee Handbook

**2023 Edition - Effective January 1, 2023**

Maintained by: Hannah Moreau, HR & Office Manager
Approved by: Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer

---

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Larkspur Ridge IT. Dana Whitfield founded Larkspur in March 2014 after years of managing IT in a hospital, with a simple idea: small businesses deserve IT support that is responsive, knowledgeable, and easy to reach. That idea is still our tagline: "IT that answers the phone."

We support small businesses across the Kansas City area from our office in Lenexa, Kansas. Our clients range from medical and dental offices to law firms, manufacturers, and nonprofits. Every person on our team, whether on the service desk, in the field, or in the office, plays a part in keeping our clients running.

What sets Larkspur apart is not a particular tool or product. It is the way we treat people. Clients call us because they are stuck, often in the middle of a busy day, and they want to talk to someone who listens, knows their environment, and follows through. We document our work so the next technician can pick up where we left off, we keep our promises about callbacks and appointments, and we explain things in plain language. We expect every employee to carry that standard into each call, ticket, and client visit.

This handbook describes the policies and expectations that apply to all employees. Please read it, keep it for reference, and ask Hannah Moreau or your manager if you have questions.

## 2. About This Handbook

This 2023 Edition is effective January 1, 2023. Larkspur may revise, supplement, or rescind any policy in this handbook at any time, to the extent permitted by law. Detailed operational procedures (for example, after-hours escalation) are documented in runbooks in Atlas Docs; this handbook covers employment terms.

This handbook is not a contract of employment.

## 3. At-Will Employment

Employment with Larkspur Ridge IT is at will. Either the employee or the company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Only the CEO, in a signed writing, may make any agreement that changes the at-will relationship.

## 4. Equal Employment Opportunity

Larkspur Ridge IT is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate in hiring, pay, promotion, discipline, termination, or any other term of employment on the basis of race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, sexual orientation, and gender identity), national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other legally protected characteristic.

Larkspur prohibits harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment, by or toward employees, clients, vendors, or visitors. Report concerns to your manager, to Hannah Moreau, or to Dana Whitfield. Larkspur will investigate promptly and will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Employees who need a reasonable accommodation for a disability or religious belief should contact Hannah Moreau.

## 5. Employment Classifications

- **Full-time:** regularly scheduled 40 hours per week.
- **Part-time:** regularly scheduled fewer than 30 hours per week.
- **Exempt:** salaried and not eligible for overtime under the FLSA.
- **Non-exempt (hourly):** eligible for overtime. Service desk and field technician roles are generally non-exempt.

New employees complete a 90-day introductory period, with check-ins with their manager during that time.

## 6. Hours of Work

### 6.1 Business Hours

Larkspur's business hours are **Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Central Time**, excluding company holidays. The service desk answers the support line live during business hours. Urgent issues outside business hours are handled by the on-call technician.

### 6.2 Schedules

A standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week, with an unpaid meal break. The Service Desk Lead, Greg Halvorsen, publishes the service desk schedule and arranges meal break coverage so the phones are always answered during business hours. Field technicians work schedules set by the Dispatcher based on client appointments.

### 6.3 Attendance

If you will be late or absent, notify your manager and the service desk Teams channel before the start of your shift. Field technicians must also notify the Dispatcher so appointments can be moved.

## 7. Pay and Overtime

Employees are paid biweekly by direct deposit. Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked. Working off the clock is not permitted.

Non-exempt employees are paid **1.5 times their regular rate** for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. Overtime should be approved in advance by your manager whenever possible.

All technical staff must enter time against tickets in Harbor PSA by the end of each working day. These time entries are used for client billing and must be accurate.

## 8. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 8.1 Accrual

Larkspur provides a single PTO bank for vacation, personal time, and illness. Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period. **Full-time employees in their first two years of service accrue 10 days of PTO per year.** Accrual rates for employees with longer service are listed in the HR memo "PTO accrual update" (January 3, 2023), which is available in the HR folder of the shared drive.

New employees may use accrued PTO after 30 days of employment.

### 8.2 Requests

Submit PTO requests at least two weeks in advance for planned absences. Requests are approved based on coverage. Technicians scheduled for on-call coverage must arrange a swap before PTO is approved.

### 8.3 Carryover

Unused PTO carryover is limited to five (5) days per calendar year. PTO above that amount is forfeited on December 31 unless state law requires otherwise.

## 9. Company Holidays

Larkspur observes the following paid holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Friday after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (half day; the office closes at 12:00 PM)
- Christmas Day

## 10. Other Leave

- **Bereavement:** up to three paid days for the death of an immediate family member.
- **Jury duty:** paid leave for up to ten working days per year.
- **Military leave:** provided as required by USERRA.
- **Medical and family leave:** unpaid leave as required by law or as approved by the company.

## 11. On-Call

Some client contracts include after-hours support for critical issues. Eligible technicians participate in an on-call rotation after completing their introductory period. On-call technicians must be reachable, have their company laptop and an internet connection available, and be fit to work.

On-call technicians receive an on-call stipend, and hourly employees are paid **1.5 times their regular rate** for time actually worked after hours. The after-hours escalation procedure and current rotation are maintained by the Director of Service Delivery in runbook RB-GEN-002.

## 12. Mileage and Expenses

Employees who drive a personal vehicle for company business are reimbursed at the **current IRS standard mileage rate**. Normal commuting to the office is not reimbursable. Field technicians record business trips in Harbor PSA against the related ticket.

Reasonable, pre-approved business expenses such as parking, tolls, and approved small parts for client repairs are reimbursed when submitted with receipts on the monthly expense form. Submit expense reports to your manager; approved reports are paid by the Controller in the next payroll.

## 13. Dress Code

Business casual is expected in the office. For client visits, wear a Larkspur polo or other business casual top, clean pants, and closed-toe shoes. Follow any safety rules at client sites, including safety footwear and safety glasses at manufacturing and construction clients. Wear your Larkspur ID badge at client sites.

## 14. Driving for Company Business

Employees who drive for company business must have a valid driver's license and personal auto insurance, and must consent to a motor vehicle record review. Do not use a handheld phone while driving, and never read or send messages while the vehicle is moving. Keep equipment out of sight in your vehicle, and never leave devices containing client data in a vehicle overnight. Report any accident to the Dispatcher and your manager as soon as it is safe to do so.

## 15. Technology Acceptable Use

- Use only company-issued devices for company and client work.
- Store client credentials only in LockBox Vault. Never put passwords in ticket notes, email, Teams, or documents.
- Do not install unapproved software or disable security tools.
- Lock your screen when you step away.
- Access client systems only for assigned work.
- Company systems may be monitored. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use of company systems.

## 16. Confidentiality

Our clients trust us with sensitive information, including health, legal, and financial records. Access client data only as needed for an assigned task, never copy it to personal devices or accounts, and never discuss client matters with anyone who does not need to know. All employees sign a confidentiality agreement at hire, and those obligations continue after employment ends.

Report any suspected exposure of client data, or any lost or stolen device, to your manager immediately.

## 17. Training and Certifications

Larkspur supports professional growth. With advance manager approval, Larkspur reimburses exam fees for job-related certifications upon passing, along with approved study materials, subject to an annual budget set by the CEO.

## 18. Performance Reviews

Managers hold check-ins during the introductory period and conduct an annual performance review each January. Reviews consider technical skill, quality of work, client communication, teamwork, and reliability.

## 19. Discipline

Larkspur generally follows progressive discipline: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, and termination. Larkspur may skip steps depending on the seriousness of the issue. Progressive discipline does not change at-will employment.

Conduct that may lead to discipline includes dishonesty (including falsified time or expense records), unauthorized access to client data, mishandling credentials, harassment, working while impaired, repeated absences, and unprofessional treatment of clients.

## 19A. Problem Resolution and Open Door

Larkspur wants to hear about problems early, while they are still easy to fix. If you have a concern about your schedule, workload, a coworker, a client, or a decision that affects you, start by talking with your manager. Most issues are resolved at that level.

If the matter is not resolved, or if it involves your manager, contact Hannah Moreau. Hannah may meet with you and the people involved, review records, and recommend a resolution. If needed, Hannah will bring the matter to Dana Whitfield, whose decision is final.

You will not be penalized for raising a concern in good faith. Concerns involving harassment, discrimination, or retaliation should be reported as described in Section 4 and will be investigated.

## 19B. Client Relationships

Our clients are small businesses, and our technicians often become familiar faces in their offices. Friendly, professional relationships with client staff are encouraged. However:

- Do not accept gifts from clients worth more than $50 without approval from your manager. Small items such as a holiday treat for the office are fine.
- Do not agree to perform work for a client "on the side" or outside a Larkspur ticket.
- Do not make promises about pricing, contract terms, or what is included in a client's service agreement. Refer those questions to the client's account manager.
- If a client asks you to do something that conflicts with a runbook or company policy, politely decline and escalate to your manager.

## 20. Separation

Employees who resign are asked to provide two weeks' written notice. All company property, including laptops, badges, keys, and client equipment, must be returned by the last day. System access is disabled on the last day of employment. Final pay is issued no later than the next regular payday.

## 21. Personnel Records

Larkspur keeps a personnel file for each employee, including your offer letter, signed acknowledgments, performance reviews, and any disciplinary records. Personnel files are kept confidential and are available only to HR, the CEO, and your manager as needed. Please notify Hannah Moreau promptly of any change to your name, address, phone number, emergency contact, tax withholding, or direct deposit information.

Employees may review their own personnel file by appointment with HR.

## 22. Workplace Safety

Larkspur is committed to a safe workplace, both in our office and at client sites.

- Report any workplace injury, however minor, to your manager and to HR the same day.
- Lift equipment safely. Ask for help with servers, UPS units, large printers, and other heavy items, and use a cart when one is available.
- Do not work inside network racks, ceilings, or electrical rooms at a client site unless the client has given access and the area is safe. Never work on building electrical systems; that work belongs to a licensed electrician.
- Follow each client's safety rules. On manufacturing and construction sites, wear the personal protective equipment the client requires.
- Threats or acts of violence are never tolerated. If you feel unsafe at a client site, leave and contact your manager.

## 23. Office Procedures

### 23.1 Visitors

All visitors to the Lenexa office must sign in at the front desk and be accompanied by an employee while in work areas. Client equipment dropped off for repair must be logged against a Harbor PSA ticket when it is received.

### 23.2 Office Security

The last person leaving the office at the end of the day is responsible for confirming that the doors are locked and the alarm is set. Do not prop doors open or share your door code or key card. Lost keys or cards must be reported to Hannah Moreau immediately.

### 23.3 Shared Spaces

Please keep the break room, conference room, and workbench clean. Label personal food in the refrigerator; unlabeled items are discarded on Friday afternoons. Return tools and spare parts to the workbench after use and log any parts used against the client ticket.

### 23.4 Phone Etiquette

The support line is answered "Thank you for calling Larkspur Ridge IT, this is [your name]. How can I help?" Every caller should hear a friendly, professional voice. If you cannot resolve the caller's issue, create a ticket, set expectations, and make sure the caller knows what will happen next.

## 24. Standards of Conduct

We expect every employee to:

- Be honest in all dealings with clients, coworkers, and the company.
- Treat clients and their staff with patience and respect, even when they are frustrated.
- Follow the runbooks and ticket standards in Atlas Docs, and ask for help when unsure.
- Document work completely and accurately in Harbor PSA.
- Protect client data and credentials at all times.
- Arrive on time, prepared to work, and fit for duty.

Questions about whether a particular action is appropriate should be raised with your manager before acting.
