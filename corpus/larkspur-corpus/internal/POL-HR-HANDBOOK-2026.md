## 1. Welcome and Company History

Welcome to Larkspur Ridge IT. Whether you joined us this week or have been with us for years, we are glad you are here.

Larkspur Ridge IT, LLC was founded in March 2014 by Dana Whitfield. Before starting the company, Dana spent several years as an IT manager in a hospital setting, where she learned that the technology itself is rarely the hardest part of IT. The hard part is being available, explaining things clearly, and following through. Dana started Larkspur to bring that standard of service to small businesses in the Kansas City area that could not justify a full internal IT department.

The company began with a handful of local clients and a single shared office. Today, Larkspur is a managed IT services provider (MSP) serving 40 active client organizations, ranging from roughly 10 to 120 users each, with approximately 1,290 managed users in total. Our clients include medical and dental practices, law firms, manufacturers, nonprofits, schools, property managers, financial firms, and many others across Kansas and Missouri. As of this edition, our team has grown to 22 employees working out of our Lenexa office, at client sites, and, for eligible roles, from home.

Our tagline is "IT that answers the phone." It is not a slogan we take lightly. Clients choose us because they can reach a real person who knows their environment, and because we do what we say we will do. Every role at Larkspur, from the service desk to billing to the front office, contributes to that promise.

This handbook explains how we work together, what you can expect from the company, and what the company expects from you. Please read it carefully and keep it handy. If anything is unclear, ask your manager or Hannah Moreau.

## 3. Employment Relationship

### 3.1 At-Will Employment

Employment with Larkspur Ridge IT is at will. This means that either the employee or the company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice, subject to applicable law. Nothing in this handbook, in any other company document, or in any oral statement creates a contract of employment for any specific duration or guarantees continued employment.

Only the Chief Executive Officer may enter into an agreement that alters the at-will relationship, and any such agreement must be in writing and signed by both the CEO and the employee.

### 3.2 Employment Eligibility

All employees must complete Form I-9 and provide acceptable documentation of identity and employment eligibility within three business days of their start date, as required by federal law.

### 3.3 Background Checks

Because our employees have privileged access to client systems and data, all offers of employment are contingent on a satisfactory background check. Positions that require driving for company business also require a satisfactory motor vehicle record review (see Section 14).

### 3.4 Outside Employment

Employees may hold outside employment provided it does not conflict with their Larkspur schedule, on-call obligations, or job performance, and does not compete with Larkspur's business. Providing IT services to any current Larkspur client, or to a prospective client Larkspur is actively pursuing, outside of your Larkspur role is not permitted. Employees should disclose any outside IT work to their manager before accepting it.

## 4. Equal Employment Opportunity and Anti-Harassment

### 4.1 Equal Employment Opportunity

Larkspur Ridge IT is an equal opportunity employer. We make employment decisions based on qualifications, performance, and business needs. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, sexual orientation, and gender identity), national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local law. This policy applies to recruiting, hiring, training, promotion, compensation, discipline, termination, and all other terms and conditions of employment.

### 4.2 Reasonable Accommodation

Larkspur provides reasonable accommodation to qualified individuals with disabilities and for sincerely held religious beliefs, unless doing so would create an undue hardship. Employees who need an accommodation should contact Hannah Moreau. Requests are handled confidentially.

### 4.3 Anti-Harassment

Larkspur prohibits harassment of any kind based on a protected characteristic, as well as sexual harassment. This applies to conduct by and toward employees, clients, client staff, vendors, and visitors, whether at our office, at a client site, in a vehicle, on a call, in Teams or email, or at a company event.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to, offensive jokes, slurs, threats, unwelcome physical contact, unwelcome sexual advances or comments, and the display or distribution of offensive material.

### 4.4 Reporting and Non-Retaliation

If you experience or witness conduct that may violate this policy, report it promptly to your manager, to Hannah Moreau, or to Dana Whitfield. You do not need to report to someone in your own chain of command. Larkspur will investigate reports promptly and as confidentially as possible, and will take appropriate corrective action.

Larkspur strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.

If a client or client employee harasses you, report it the same way. Your safety comes first; you may leave a client site or end a call if you feel unsafe, and then contact your manager.

## 5. Employment Classifications and Introductory Period

### 5.1 Classifications

- **Full-time:** regularly scheduled to work 40 hours per week.
- **Part-time:** regularly scheduled to work fewer than 30 hours per week.
- **Exempt:** salaried employees who are not eligible for overtime under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), based on job duties and salary.
- **Non-exempt (hourly):** employees who are eligible for overtime pay. Most technician roles at Larkspur, including Tier 1, Tier 2, and Field Technicians, as well as the Dispatcher / Service Coordinator, are classified as non-exempt.

Your classification is stated in your offer letter. Questions about classification should go to Hannah Moreau.

### 5.2 Introductory Period

The first 90 calendar days of employment are an introductory period. During this time, you and your manager will evaluate whether the role is a good fit. Your manager will hold check-ins at approximately 30, 60, and 90 days. Completion of the introductory period does not change the at-will nature of employment.

Some policies, such as eligibility for remote work (Section 7) and the on-call rotation (Section 12), begin only after the introductory period.

## 6. Hours of Work and Shift Coverage

### 6.1 Business Hours

Larkspur's business hours are **Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central Time (CT)**, excluding company holidays (Section 10). All times in this handbook and in company systems are US Central Time.

Business hours are the hours during which our support line, (913) 555-0111, is answered live by the service desk and during which business-hours SLA clocks run for client tickets. Outside business hours, urgent client issues are handled through the on-call rotation (Section 12).

### 6.2 Standard Workweek

The standard workweek runs from 12:00 AM Monday through 11:59 PM Sunday. A standard full-time schedule is 40 hours per week, generally eight hours per day plus an unpaid meal break.

### 6.3 Technician Shift Coverage

Because our business day is eleven hours long, the service desk is staffed in overlapping shifts. The Service Desk Manager publishes the shift schedule at least two weeks in advance. Standard shifts are:

| Shift | Hours (CT) | Typical staffing |
|---|---|---|
| Early | 7:00 AM - 3:30 PM | At least two technicians, including one Tier 2 |
| Mid | 8:30 AM - 5:00 PM | Remaining service desk staff |
| Late | 9:30 AM - 6:00 PM | At least two technicians, including one Tier 2 |

Each shift includes a 30-minute unpaid meal break, scheduled so that the desk is never left without coverage. Field Technicians and the Dispatcher work schedules set by the Dispatcher and the Field/Onsite Lead based on client appointments, generally within business hours.

Technicians on the Early shift are expected to be logged into Harbor PSA and the phone system, ready to take calls, at 7:00 AM. Technicians on the Late shift may not log off before 6:00 PM unless the Service Desk Manager or the shift's Tier 2 lead approves.

### 6.4 Schedule Changes and Shift Swaps

Employees may swap shifts with a coworker of the same tier with advance approval from the Service Desk Manager. Swaps must be recorded on the shared shift calendar. Requests should be made at least 48 hours in advance except in emergencies.

### 6.5 Attendance and Punctuality

Reliable attendance is essential to our service. If you will be late or absent, notify your manager and the service desk Teams channel as early as possible, and no later than the start of your shift. Field Technicians must also notify the Dispatcher so client appointments can be rescheduled.

Two consecutive scheduled shifts missed without notice may be treated as a voluntary resignation.

### 6.6 Meal and Rest Breaks

In addition to the unpaid meal break, non-exempt employees may take short paid rest breaks (generally up to 15 minutes) during each four-hour work period, coordinated so that phone coverage is maintained.

## 7. Remote Work

### 7.1 Eligibility for Technicians

**Tier 1 and Tier 2 technicians may work remotely up to two (2) days per week after completing 90 days of employment.** Remote days must be scheduled in advance with the Service Desk Manager and recorded on the shift calendar. The Service Desk Manager may limit remote days during periods of high volume, staffing shortages, training, or client projects that require onsite presence.

### 7.2 Other Roles

Field Technicians work primarily at client sites and in the office and are not eligible for scheduled remote days, though they may complete documentation remotely at the Dispatcher's direction. Remote arrangements for other roles are at the discretion of the employee's manager and the CEO and must be documented in writing.

### 7.3 Remote Work Requirements

Employees working remotely must:

- Work from a private, quiet location within the Kansas City metropolitan area unless otherwise approved.
- Use only their Larkspur-issued laptop for company and client work.
- Connect through the company VPN when required for access to internal systems.
- Be reachable by phone and Teams for their full scheduled shift, with the same responsiveness expected in the office.
- Keep client information out of view of household members and others, and never print client documents at home.
- Be available to come into the office or travel to a client site with reasonable notice if business needs require it.

Remote work is a privilege, not an entitlement. Eligibility may be suspended if performance, attendance, or ticket quality falls below expectations.

## 8. Timekeeping, Pay, and Overtime

### 8.1 Pay Schedule

Employees are paid biweekly by direct deposit. Pay stubs are available through the payroll portal. Questions about pay should go to Samuel Ortiz, Controller, or to Hannah Moreau.

### 8.2 Timekeeping

Non-exempt employees must accurately record all hours worked in the timekeeping system, including time spent on after-hours calls. Separately, all technical staff record client work as time entries against tickets in Harbor PSA; these entries are used for client billing and service reporting and must be accurate and timely (entered no later than the end of each working day).

Working "off the clock" is prohibited. Supervisors may not ask or allow any non-exempt employee to work without recording the time. Falsifying time records is grounds for termination.

### 8.3 Overtime for Hourly Staff

Non-exempt employees are paid overtime at **one and one-half times (1.5x) their regular rate** for all hours worked over 40 in a workweek, in accordance with the FLSA.

- Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager whenever possible.
- Work performed while on call outside business hours (Section 12) is paid at 1.5x for hourly staff regardless of total weekly hours, as described in Section 12.3.
- Working unapproved overtime may result in discipline, but all hours worked will always be paid.

Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay. However, exempt employees who serve on the on-call rotation receive the on-call stipend described in Section 12.

### 8.4 Payroll Deductions

Larkspur makes deductions required by law and deductions you authorize, such as benefit premiums. If you believe an improper deduction was made, report it to Samuel Ortiz or Hannah Moreau, and it will be investigated and corrected promptly.

## 9. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 9.1 Overview

Larkspur provides a single bank of paid time off (PTO) for vacation, personal time, and illness. Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on length of service.

### 9.2 Accrual Schedule

| Length of service | Annual PTO | Accrual per biweekly pay period (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 - 2 years | **15 days** (120 hours) | 4.62 hours |
| 3 - 5 years | **20 days** (160 hours) | 6.15 hours |
| 6 or more years | **25 days** (200 hours) | 7.69 hours |

New employees begin accruing PTO on their first day and may use accrued PTO after their first 30 days of employment. The higher accrual rate begins with the pay period that includes the employee's service anniversary (for example, an employee hired in June 2023 moves to the 3-5 year rate in June 2026).

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis according to their regularly scheduled hours.

### 9.3 Carryover

Employees may carry over a **maximum of five (5) days (40 hours)** of unused PTO into the next calendar year. PTO above the carryover limit that remains unused at the end of the calendar year (December 31) is forfeited, except where state law requires otherwise. Larkspur does not pay out unused PTO in lieu of time off during employment.

### 9.4 Requesting PTO

- Submit PTO requests through the HR scheduling system at least two weeks in advance for planned absences of one day or more.
- Technicians must also confirm that their absence does not conflict with their scheduled on-call week. If it does, the technician must arrange a swap before the PTO is approved (see Section 12.5).
- Requests are approved on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to coverage. The Service Desk Manager may limit the number of technicians off at one time.
- PTO for illness should be reported as early as possible, as described in Section 6.5.

### 9.5 PTO at Separation

Accrued, unused PTO is paid at separation only as described in Section 21.4.

2. Memorial Day

## 12. On-Call Rotation and Stipend

### 12.1 Purpose

Many of our clients have contracts that include 24x7 support for critical issues. Professional tier clients receive 24x7 coverage for P1 (Critical) incidents, and Premier tier clients receive 24x7 coverage for all priorities. To meet these commitments, Larkspur maintains an after-hours on-call rotation.

### 12.2 Rotation and Expectations

- The on-call rotation is staffed **weekly** by Tier 2 and Tier 3 engineers. The current rotation, handoff time, and detailed procedure are maintained by the Director of Service Delivery in OPS-ONCALL-2026 and runbook RB-GEN-002 in Atlas Docs.
- After-hours calls reach the on-call engineer through **PagerLine at (913) 555-0142**, which pages the engineer on rotation.
- The on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within **15 minutes**. If the on-call engineer does not answer within 15 minutes, PagerLine escalates to Tom Kessler, Senior Systems Engineer, and then to Marcus Oyelaran, Director of Service Delivery.
- While on call, the engineer must remain within reasonable reach of their company laptop and a reliable internet connection, remain fit to work (no alcohol or other impairing substances), and keep their phone charged and audible.
- The on-call engineer must document all after-hours work in Harbor PSA tickets in accordance with ticket quality standards.

Employees are added to the rotation after completing their introductory period and the on-call training checklist.

### 12.3 On-Call Compensation

- **On-call stipend:** Employees receive **$300 per week** for each full week they serve on call. Partial weeks (for example, due to an approved swap) are prorated by day.
- **After-hours time for hourly staff:** Non-exempt employees are paid at **1.5x** their regular rate for all time actually worked while on call outside business hours, including time spent on calls, remote sessions, and travel to a client site. Time is recorded in 15-minute increments with a minimum of 15 minutes per incident.
- The stipend is paid in addition to, and is not a substitute for, after-hours pay owed to hourly staff.

### 12.4 Holidays on Call

An employee who is on call over a company holiday receives the regular stipend and holiday pay. Hourly staff are paid 1.5x for time worked on the holiday in addition to holiday pay.

### 12.5 Swaps and Coverage

Employees may swap on-call weeks or days with another engineer on the rotation. Swaps must be approved by the Director of Service Delivery and updated in PagerLine before the swap begins. The employee originally scheduled remains responsible for coverage until the PagerLine schedule has been updated.

## 13. Travel, Mileage, and Expense Reimbursement

### 13.1 Mileage Reimbursement

Employees who use a personal vehicle for company business, such as traveling to client sites or picking up equipment, are reimbursed at the **current IRS standard mileage rate** in effect on the date of travel.

- Mileage is reimbursable from the office (or from your home, if shorter) to the client site and back, and between client sites.
- Normal commuting between your home and the Lenexa office is not reimbursable.
- **Field Technicians log every business trip in Harbor PSA** against the related ticket or dispatch, including the starting point, destination, and miles driven. Other employees submit mileage on the monthly expense report.
- Mileage must be submitted within 30 days of the trip.

### 13.2 Reimbursable Business Expenses

Larkspur reimburses reasonable, necessary, and pre-approved business expenses, including:

- Parking and tolls incurred on company business.
- Small parts or cables purchased for an urgent client repair when the Dispatcher or a manager approves the purchase in advance (the receipt must be attached to the client ticket so the item can be billed if appropriate).
- Meals during approved overnight travel or when a client engagement requires working through a meal period away from the office, up to $50 per day.
- Approved training and certification costs (Section 18).
- Cell phone allowance for employees on the on-call rotation, as stated in the employee's offer letter or role memo.

### 13.3 Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Larkspur does not reimburse traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, alcohol (unless pre-approved for a client event by the CEO), personal vehicle repairs or insurance, or expenses submitted without a receipt (other than mileage).

### 13.4 Submitting Expenses

Submit expense reports monthly using the expense form in the shared drive, with itemized receipts, to your manager for approval. Approved reports are forwarded to Samuel Ortiz for payment in the next regular payroll. Expenses must be submitted within 60 days of being incurred.

### 13.5 Company Cards

A limited number of company purchase cards are issued to specific roles. Cardholders must follow the purchase card guidelines issued by the Controller.

## 14. Vehicle Use and Driving Rules for Field Staff

### 14.1 Applicability

This section applies to Field Technicians and to any employee who drives for company business, including technicians who make occasional onsite visits.

### 14.2 License and Insurance Requirements

Employees who drive for company business must:

- Hold a valid driver's license for their state of residence.
- Maintain personal auto liability insurance at or above the state minimum, and provide proof of insurance to HR at hire and at each renewal.
- Consent to a motor vehicle record (MVR) review at hire and annually thereafter.
- Report any license suspension, revocation, or moving violation conviction to HR within five days.

An employee whose MVR shows a serious violation (such as driving under the influence or reckless driving) within the past three years may not drive for company business.

### 14.3 Safe Driving Rules

- Obey all traffic laws and posted speed limits.
- **Do not use a handheld phone while driving.** Do not read or send texts, Teams messages, or emails, or review tickets while the vehicle is moving. Hands-free calls should be brief; pull over safely for anything that requires attention.
- Do not drive while impaired by alcohol, drugs, medication, or fatigue.
- Wear a seat belt at all times, and require all passengers to do the same.
- Do not transport non-employee passengers on company business without manager approval.

### 14.4 Equipment in Vehicles

- Client equipment and company hardware must be kept out of sight (in the trunk or a covered cargo area) and must never be left in an unattended vehicle overnight.
- Laptops, drives, and other devices containing client data must not be left in a vehicle unattended for longer than necessary to complete a stop. If a device is lost or stolen, report it immediately to your manager and to Renee Castillo, who follows RB-SEC-002.
- Secure loose equipment so it cannot shift while driving.

### 14.5 Accidents

If you are involved in an accident while on company business:

1. Make sure everyone is safe and call 911 if anyone is injured.
2. Contact the police as required by law.
3. Exchange information with the other driver(s) but do not admit fault.
4. Notify the Dispatcher and your manager as soon as it is safe to do so.
5. Submit a written incident report to HR within 24 hours.

### 14.6 Fines

Parking tickets, traffic citations, and toll violations incurred while driving for company business are the responsibility of the driver.

## 16. Technology Acceptable Use

### 16.1 Company Equipment

Larkspur issues laptops, phones (where applicable), headsets, and peripherals according to the current company hardware standard (STD-HW-2026). Company equipment remains company property and must be returned at separation.

- Use only company-issued devices to access client systems, Harbor PSA, Beacon RMM, Atlas Docs, LockBox Vault, and other internal tools.
- Do not install unapproved software on company devices. Request software through your manager or the internal IT request process.
- Keep devices patched, encrypted, and protected by the endpoint protection installed by Larkspur. Do not disable security tools.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.

### 16.2 Accounts and Credentials

- Your Larkspur Microsoft 365 account must be protected by multi-factor authentication using Microsoft Authenticator.
- **All client credentials must be stored only in LockBox Vault.** Never store client passwords in Harbor PSA ticket notes, Atlas Docs pages, email, Teams messages, sticky notes, spreadsheets, or personal password managers.
- Never share your own credentials with anyone, including coworkers.
- Use the minimum level of access needed to complete a task, and use named administrator accounts rather than shared accounts wherever the client environment allows.

### 16.3 Acceptable and Prohibited Use

Limited personal use of company technology is permitted if it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or violate any policy. Prohibited uses include:

- Accessing, downloading, or sharing illegal, sexually explicit, or harassing material.
- Using company or client systems for personal commercial activity.
- Accessing client systems, mailboxes, or files for any reason other than an assigned ticket, project, or approved maintenance task.
- Attempting to bypass security controls on company or client systems.
- Using personal cloud storage, personal email, or personal AI tools to store, process, or transmit client data.

### 16.4 Generative AI Tools

Employees may use AI tools that Larkspur has approved for internal use (the current list is maintained in Atlas Docs). Do not paste client data, credentials, or confidential information into any AI tool that has not been approved for that purpose. You remain responsible for the accuracy of anything you send to a client or record in a ticket, regardless of how it was drafted.

### 16.5 Monitoring

Company systems, including email, Teams, Harbor PSA, Beacon RMM sessions, phone recordings, and company devices, are company property and may be monitored, logged, and reviewed for quality, security, and compliance purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use of company systems, to the extent permitted by law.

### 16.6 Social Media

Do not post client names, client logos, photos of client sites or screens, or details of client incidents on social media. Only the CEO or a designated employee may speak on behalf of Larkspur publicly.

## 17. Confidentiality of Client and Company Information

### 17.1 Our Duty to Clients

Our clients trust us with access to their most sensitive systems and information, including patient records at healthcare clients, legal matters at law firms, financial data at accounting and advisory firms, and personnel data at nearly every client. Protecting that information is a condition of employment at Larkspur.

### 17.2 Confidential Information

Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:

- Client data of any kind, including files, emails, databases, and anything viewed during a remote session.
- Client network diagrams, configurations, credentials, and security settings.
- Client lists, contract terms, pricing, and renewal information.
- Larkspur's internal documentation, runbooks, financial information, and employee information.

### 17.3 Handling Rules

- Access client information only as needed to perform an assigned task.
- Do not discuss client matters in public places or with anyone who does not need to know, including family and friends.
- Do not copy client data to removable media, personal devices, or personal accounts.
- Do not open, read, or search client email or documents beyond what a ticket requires. If you accidentally see sensitive information (for example, while restoring a file), do not read further, do not disclose it, and complete the task.
- Healthcare clients' data may include protected health information. Employees must complete Larkspur's annual privacy training, and must follow any additional requirements stated in the client's agreement.
- Report any suspected or actual exposure of client data, or a misdirected email, immediately to your manager and to Marcus Oyelaran. Report a lost or stolen device immediately to your manager and to Renee Castillo, who follows RB-SEC-002.

### 17.4 Confidentiality Agreement

All employees sign a confidentiality and invention assignment agreement at hire. Obligations under that agreement continue after employment ends.

## 18. Training and Certification Reimbursement

### 18.1 Commitment to Growth

Our clients depend on our technical skill, and the technology changes constantly. Larkspur encourages every technical employee to keep learning and to earn industry certifications that support their role.

### 18.2 Eligible Programs

With advance written approval from your manager, Larkspur reimburses:

- Exam fees for job-related certifications (for example, CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+; Microsoft 365 and Azure certifications such as MS-102 and MD-102; Fortinet NSE certifications; and Ubiquiti certifications).
- Official study materials or online training subscriptions approved by your manager.
- Approved vendor training and conferences.

### 18.3 Limits and Conditions

- Employees must be employed and in good standing at the time of reimbursement.
- If an employee voluntarily resigns within twelve (12) months after receiving reimbursement for a single program totaling more than $1,000, the employee agrees to repay a prorated portion of that reimbursement, as stated in the training agreement signed before enrollment.

### 18.4 Study Time

Technicians may use up to two hours per week of scheduled work time for approved study, scheduled with the Service Desk Manager so that coverage is maintained. Time spent taking an approved certification exam during a scheduled shift is paid.

### 18.5 Recognition

Employees who earn a certification on the company's role development list receive recognition at the next all-hands meeting and may become eligible for a pay adjustment as part of the annual review.

## 19. Performance Reviews

### 19.1 Review Cycle

- **Introductory check-ins** at approximately 30, 60, and 90 days.
- **Mid-year check-in** in July: an informal conversation about goals, development, and any concerns.
- **Annual performance review** in January, covering the prior calendar year. Annual compensation adjustments, if any, take effect with the first full pay period in February.

### 19.2 What We Evaluate

Reviews consider job knowledge and technical skill, quality and accuracy of work, client communication, teamwork, reliability, and adherence to company standards. For service desk and field staff, reviews also consider metrics described in the service desk quality guide (OPS-QUALITY-2025), including ticket hygiene, client satisfaction (CSAT) feedback, first-contact resolution, and SLA performance. Metrics are one input into the review, not the whole review.

### 19.3 Self-Assessment and Goals

Before the annual review, each employee completes a short self-assessment. The employee and manager agree on goals for the coming year, including at least one professional development goal.

### 19.4 Ongoing Feedback

Managers are expected to give feedback throughout the year, not only during formal reviews. Employees are encouraged to raise questions or concerns with their manager at any time.

## 20. Standards of Conduct and Discipline

### 20.1 Standards of Conduct

Employees are expected to act honestly, treat clients and coworkers with respect, follow company policies and runbooks, and protect client trust. Examples of conduct that may result in discipline, up to and including termination, include:

- Dishonesty, including falsifying time entries, mileage, expense reports, or ticket notes.
- Accessing client systems or data without a business reason.
- Mishandling client credentials or confidential information.
- Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, or violence.
- Working while impaired by alcohol or drugs, or possessing illegal drugs on company or client premises.
- Repeated tardiness, unexcused absences, or failing to respond while on call.
- Rude or unprofessional treatment of clients.
- Theft or intentional damage to company or client property.
- Insubordination.
- Violations of the vehicle and driving rules.

This list is not exhaustive.

### 20.2 Progressive Discipline

Larkspur generally uses progressive discipline to correct performance or conduct issues:

1. **Verbal warning**, documented in the employee's file.
2. **Written warning**, signed by the employee and manager.
3. **Final written warning**, which may include a performance improvement plan (PIP) of 30 to 60 days.
4. **Termination of employment.**

Larkspur may skip steps or move directly to termination depending on the seriousness of the conduct. Progressive discipline does not change the at-will employment relationship.

### 20.3 Investigations

Larkspur may place an employee on paid administrative leave while investigating a serious concern. Employees are expected to cooperate fully and honestly with any investigation.

### 20.4 Problem Resolution

If you have a concern about a work-related decision, first discuss it with your manager. If the matter is not resolved, or if it involves your manager, contact Hannah Moreau, who may involve the CEO.

## 21. Separation from Employment

### 21.1 Resignation

Employees who resign are asked to give at least two (2) weeks' written notice to their manager. Employees on the on-call rotation should also notify the Director of Service Delivery so the rotation can be adjusted.

### 21.2 Return of Property

On or before the last day of employment, employees must return all company property, including laptops, phones, headsets, ID badges, keys and access cards, company shirts in good condition (optional), and any client equipment in their possession. Final expense reimbursements may be withheld until all property is returned, to the extent permitted by law.

### 21.3 Access Removal

On the employee's last day, the Director of Service Delivery or a designee will disable the employee's access to company systems, including Microsoft 365, Harbor PSA, Beacon RMM, Atlas Docs, LockBox Vault, and PagerLine, and will rotate any shared client credentials the employee had access to. The employee's name will be removed from escalation lists and on-call schedules. Managers are responsible for making sure any document that lists the departing employee as a contact is updated.

### 21.4 Final Pay and PTO Payout

Final pay is issued no later than the next regular payday following the last day of work. Employees who resign with at least two weeks' notice and work through their notice period are paid for accrued, unused PTO, up to a maximum of 80 hours. Employees who are terminated for misconduct, or who resign without notice, are not eligible for PTO payout except where required by law.

### 21.5 Exit Interview

Departing employees are invited to an exit interview with Hannah Moreau. Feedback is used to improve the workplace and is kept confidential.

### 21.6 References

All reference requests must be referred to Hannah Moreau. Larkspur will confirm dates of employment and job title only, unless the former employee authorizes additional information in writing.
