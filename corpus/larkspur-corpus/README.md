# Larkspur Ridge IT - Course Corpus

Larkspur Ridge IT is a **fictional** managed IT services provider in Lenexa, Kansas, with 24 staff and 40 small-business clients. Every person, company, phone number, and address here is made up. You'll use this company's documents and data in most labs from Module 4 onward, and its help-desk assistant grows across the semester.

## What's inside

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `client-docs/` | What Larkspur gives its clients: service catalog, SLA, price sheet, FAQ, onboarding guide, announcements, newsletters |
| `runbooks/` | Technician procedures from the Atlas Docs knowledge base (some runbooks have more than one version) |
| `internal/` | Employee handbooks, hardware standards, on-call guide, quality guide, tool release notes, meeting notes, org chart |
| `data/` | SQLite database + CSVs: clients, contacts, technicians, assets, sla_targets, and ~1,500 support tickets. Start with `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` |

Each document begins with front matter (`doc_id`, `title`, `doc_type`, `version`, `effective_date`, `owner`, `audience`).

## Like any real company's files...

...these are not perfectly consistent. Documents were written at different times, and not everything old was cleaned up. Part of your job in the labs is to notice when sources disagree and decide which one to trust. Pay attention to dates and versions.

"Today" in Larkspur's world is **Monday, November 2, 2026**.
