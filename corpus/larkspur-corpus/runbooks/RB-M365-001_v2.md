# RB-M365-001 Mailbox full / archive setup

## Purpose / When to use

Use when a user gets "Your mailbox is almost full" or "Your mailbox is full" warnings, cannot send mail, or when a client asks for an online archive. Also use when Beacon RMM or the monthly M365 report flags mailboxes over 90% of quota.

## Background

- Business Standard and Business Premium mailboxes have a **50 GB** primary mailbox quota.
- Exchange Online issues a warning at about 49 GB, prohibits send at 49.5 GB, and prohibits send/receive at 50 GB.
- **Business Standard** includes a basic online archive (50 GB).
- **Business Premium** includes Exchange Online Archiving: archive up to 1.5 TB with **auto-expanding archiving**.
- Moving mail to the archive is done by a retention policy (MRM) with a "Move to archive" tag. Our standard tenant policy is **LR-Default MRM**, which moves items older than 2 years to the archive. Some clients use a different age; check the client page.

## Prerequisites / access needed

- Exchange admin center via GDAP (Exchange Administrator)
- Exchange Online PowerShell (`Connect-ExchangeOnline`)
- Remote session to the user's PC for Outlook-side cleanup

## Steps

1. **Confirm the size.** Exchange admin center > **Recipients > Mailboxes** > user > **Mailbox usage**. Or:
   ```powershell
   Connect-ExchangeOnline -UserPrincipalName admin@client.example
   Get-MailboxStatistics jdoe@client.example | Format-List DisplayName,TotalItemSize,ItemCount
   Get-Mailbox jdoe@client.example | Format-List ProhibitSendQuota,ArchiveStatus,RetentionPolicy
   ```
2. **Quick relief** if the user cannot send right now:
   - Empty **Deleted Items** and **Junk Email**.
   - Check **Recoverable Items** size: `Get-MailboxFolderStatistics jdoe@client.example -FolderScope RecoverableItems | ft Name,FolderSize`. A large Recoverable Items folder (often from litigation hold or retention) counts against a separate 30 GB / 100 GB quota, not the primary; if it is huge, escalate.
   - In Outlook, sort Inbox and Sent Items by size and help the user delete or save large attachments to OneDrive.
3. **Enable the archive** if not already enabled:
   - EAC > Recipients > Mailboxes > user > **Others > Manage mailbox archive** > Enable.
   - Or: `Enable-Mailbox -Identity jdoe@client.example -Archive`
4. **Business Premium only:** enable auto-expanding archiving (tenant-wide or per user):
   ```powershell
   Enable-Mailbox -Identity jdoe@client.example -AutoExpandingArchive
   ```
   Auto-expanding cannot be turned off once enabled. Do not enable it on Business Standard mailboxes.
5. **Confirm the retention policy** is assigned:
   ```powershell
   Set-Mailbox jdoe@client.example -RetentionPolicy "LR-Default MRM"
   Start-ManagedFolderAssistant -Identity jdoe@client.example
   ```
   The Managed Folder Assistant can take up to 7 days to fully process a large mailbox on its own; running it manually speeds that up but it still may take 24-48 hours to see the primary shrink.
6. **Tell the user what to expect:** an **Online Archive - name** folder appears in Outlook (classic and new Outlook) and in Outlook on the web. Archived mail is searchable but not cached offline in classic Outlook.
7. **Check the OST** in classic Outlook if the user's PC is slow: a 40 GB+ OST makes Outlook sluggish. Adjust **File > Account Settings > Account Settings > Change > Mail to keep offline** to 1 year. See RB-M365-003 if the profile is damaged.
8. **Verify** in 24-48 hours that the primary mailbox has dropped below 90%. Leave the ticket open in Waiting status with a follow-up date.

## Shared mailboxes

Shared mailboxes are free up to 50 GB. A shared mailbox needs a license (Exchange Online Plan 2 or Plan 1 with Exchange Online Archiving) to enable an archive or exceed 50 GB. Licensing changes need authorized contact approval; send to the account manager.

## Priority guidance

- Warning only, user can send and receive: **P3** (or P4 if the client asked for an archive proactively).
- User cannot send or receive: **P3**; **P2** if the user is a VIP or the mailbox is a key shared mailbox (front desk, orders, billing).
- Many mailboxes full at once: likely a retention or hold change; escalate to Renee Castillo as **P2**.

## Escalate if

- The mailbox is on litigation hold or a retention hold, or Recoverable Items is over 25 GB: Renee Castillo.
- The archive is over 1 TB, or auto-expanding archive is not provisioning: Renee Castillo.
- The client wants a different retention age or a tenant-wide policy change: Renee Castillo (change needs client approval).
- License purchase needed: account manager.

## Ticket notes

- Mailbox size before and after, archive status, retention policy assigned
- Quick-relief actions taken
- Follow-up date to confirm the primary dropped
- Any licensing change and approver
